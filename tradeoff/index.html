Notes

* Flight fares are estimates, taken in November 2026 for travel in January 2027 (half of the average round-trip fare for nonstop flights on 20–23 January, from Google Flights; the average of the cheapest and median fares). Fares change daily.

CO₂ only. These figures count the fuel burned (or electricity used) on the trip. They leave out building the vehicles and, for flights, contrails and other effects at altitude, so the flight figures are conservative.

Occupancy assumed. Train: the India GHG Program's average of 7.837 g CO₂ per passenger-km for non-suburban trains in 2014–15. It divides the fuel and electricity the railways used by the passenger-km actually travelled that year, so it reflects the average occupancy of trains then, not a set number of passengers. Sleeper bus: 75% full, so 27 passengers in a 36-berth bus. Car: either 1 person or 4 people sharing. Fuller trains and buses emit less per person; emptier ones emit more.
